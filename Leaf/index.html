About this drawing

Present-day, generalised physical geography. No political boundaries are drawn. This is a small authored leaf, not a navigational chart or a complete atlas.

The wind strokes, skiff, sounds and moving creature are imaginative marks on the page. They are not weather, transport or wildlife observations. Paper light changes the illustration; it does not set a real date or hour.

Coasts: Natural Earth, 1:10 million, public domain. Relief and approximate soundings: NASA’s GEBCO 2008 grayscale imagery, generalised for this drawing. Lettering: Libertinus Serif, SIL Open Font License. Artwork and sound synthesis are original to this leaf.
