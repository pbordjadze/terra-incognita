# Pando: critics' log

One block a round, newest last (painting-company `process/critic.md`).

## Round 1 · 2026-11-13 ET · build: 8ebeb66 · critic: fresh subagent

- First impression: the dusk sky and ridges work at once; the grove below is a wall of flat gold discs (confetti), its turned leaves cream pills; a gust makes it messier; the stem a real step up, though its scars stick out like ruler ticks; the leaf beautiful, but its stalk stops short of the blade and reads round; the skin lovely, the shade closing the pores clearly.
- Weakest thing: the opening grove, flat dot-confetti, the weakest picture in the toy.
- The one change: crowns that read as masses of leaves in evening light, a lit side and a shaded side, fading into the haze; a gust a pale shimmer crossing them.
- Ship as is? No.
- Done for round 2: each crown a shaded body with its leaves at the surface lit from the low sun on one side; stronger haze with distance; a turned leaf a small pale glint; the bark one stroke a layer with lens-shaped eyes inside it; the blade moved up to meet its stalk, and the stalk a flat ribbon that thins as it twists.

## Round 2 · 2026-11-13 ET · build: cc0cd1a · critic: fresh subagent

- First impression: the sky still lands; the grove better (haze, a shaded side) but still stacked gold discs, now on brown blobs, with blurry halos between the crowns; the white trunks barely show; a gust is cream dots scattered everywhere (round 1's point back in a new shape). The stalk now meets the blade (fixed) but reads beaded, not flat. The skin and the shade still clear.
- Weakest thing: the opening grove, the first picture seen.
- The one change: make it read as aspens: pale trunks rising through it, crowns as soft masses of light rather than countable discs; a gust a pale shimmer crossing, not confetti.
- Ship as is? No.
- Done for round 3: rounder, smaller crowns with many more, softer-edged leaf clusters that melt into masses; each stem drawn on up inside its crown, white in the gaps; the crowns' bodies without halos; a turned leaf a smaller, brighter glint; a finger's gust narrower and gentler. (A view from under the crowns was tried and dropped: a few big trees, no grove.) The stalk's beaded edge line is gone.

## Round 3 · 2026-11-13 ET · build: d612307 · critic: fresh subagent

- First impression: the pale trunks finally show (round 2's point fixed), but the grove reads as an orchard of gold lollipops in planted rows; the gust still lights dots everywhere at once rather than a wave crossing (back from rounds 1–2); the stem an aspen, its leaves coins on pins, the scars evenly spaced; the leaf glowing, its stalk still beaded and thick; the skin and its shade the best moment, "beautiful, a surprise, readable with no explanation".
- Weakest thing: the grove, stylised rows rather than a wild stand.
- The one change: a wild stand, uneven, crowded, overlapping, crowns as shimmering masses; a gust a band of paleness travelling across.
- Ship as is? No.
- Done after round 3: stems scattered at random at the real density (about one to nine square metres), of mixed heights, each leaning its own way; a passing gust pales and brightens whole crowns as well as turning leaves, so it shows as a band moving over the grove (checked in a film of the grove, 3 s apart); the stalk one smooth ribbon, thinner; the bark's eyes fewer, of uneven sizes.
- **Called done for this build (maker, three rounds, process/critic.md):** round 3's one change is made and checked by eye on stills and a film. What is left is for Paul in hand: the motion itself (the gusts, the leaves' twist), the dives' feel, and the sound, none of which stills can judge.
