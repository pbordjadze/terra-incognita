## Round 1 · 2026-11-13 01:20 ET · build: branch claude/project-thread-0qnxke (uncommitted) · critic: fresh subagent

- First impression: a dark, flat wood like a stage set; the bottom half an empty floor; no fireflies at first; a burst is a faint band of greenish dashes low on the horizon.
- Weakest thing: the fireflies are too few, too dim, too small and too far; the wood never lights up together; flashes read as dashes.
- The one change: make the synchronous burst overwhelming and close: hundreds of bright points at every depth, the whole frame blinking on and then truly black, the trees silhouetted by the flash.
- Ship as is? No.
- Done for round 2: 1,100 males (from 420) at every depth and up into the trees, most within a metre or so of the ground; brighter, larger points with no streak; the night darker between bursts and the haze and canopy lit green by a burst, so the trunks stand dark against it; a narrower, taller view; the females' answer lights the litter round them.

## Round 2 · 2026-11-13 02:05 ET · build: branch claude/project-thread-0qnxke (uncommitted) · critic: fresh subagent

- First impression: dusk reads as a dark wood with a few specks, the canopy a camouflage of flat blue blotches; the burst turns the whole screen green and lands ("the thing Paul will want to show people"); then truly dark.
- Weakest thing: the burst is a screen-wide green filter, not thousands of lights in a real wood: every firefly the same size near or far; the trunks stop in mid-air during the flash and the canopy vanishes; the bottom 40% an empty floor again, where the finger's light and the females' answer happen.
- The one change: depth all the way to the viewer: near fireflies big, soft and bright, far ones small; the flash lights the trees and the litter, keeping them dark shapes, the females' ground visible.
- Ship as is? No.
- Done for round 3: near fireflies larger and out of focus, far ones small; trunks run up out of the frame; the burst lights the canopy's leaves and the litter rather than only the air; the light's flash lights the litter under it; a finer canopy.

## Round 3 · 2026-11-13 02:35 ET · build: branch claude/project-thread-0qnxke (uncommitted) · critic: fresh subagent

- First impression: a quiet, believable dark wood; trunks ending in rounded caps in mid-air; the burst "beautiful at once" with depth (near big and soft, far small and sharp: round 2's depth point fixed); above the shrubs a flat green wash; below the near fireflies an empty floor.
- Weakest thing: the bottom 40%, where the finger's light and the females' answer happen, is still a featureless floor: no litter, ferns or trail edge for the light to fall on.
- The one change: make the near ground a real place, litter and ferns to the screen's foot as dark shapes the burst, the light and the females' doublet catch on.
- Ship as is? No.
- Done after round 3: ferns along the trail's edges to the screen's foot, leaf litter in flecks the light catches, the burst lighting the litter near the watcher; trunks drawn in short lengths graded from the haze at the foot to dark above (no caps); the green in the air above the shrubs halved.
