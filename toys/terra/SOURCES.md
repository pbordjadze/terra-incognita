# Terra Incognita: sources

What is measured, what is drawn, and from where. Each toy Terra holds has its own (`toys/<toy>/SOURCES.md`);
inside Terra they are the same toys, unchanged.

## Measured

- **The Earth**: the kit's globe (toy-kit `globe`), the month's ground from NASA's Blue Marble in Terra's
  light key, the sea by depth (GEBCO), the month's sea ice; by night the Black Marble's lights. The month
  is the reader's own (the device's clock).
- **The places of the lights**: where each toy opens. Whales: the middle of the two whales on their real
  routes (`toys/whales/data/routes.json`), moving along them as the light shows them. Murmuration: Ham
  Wall, Somerset (the toy's `PLACE`). Mold: between Europe's cities (the toy opens over Europe). Fireflies:
  the eastern United States' cities round the Great Smoky Mountains (the toy opens over the Americas; its
  rule credits the Smokies' synchronous fireflies). Bell: Valdivia, Chile, 1960 (the first of the toy's
  great quakes). Swell: the mid-Pacific where the toy opens. Battle: Cannae, Apulia.
- **The cities in the mold's and the fireflies' lights**: their real longitudes and latitudes.

## Drawn

- Every light's likeness of its toy: the whales' song-rings, a small flock, threads growing between cities,
  sixteen cities' clocks falling into step (the fireflies' own rule, small), a ring crossing the Earth at no
  real speed, a train of crests, an engraving's two inks closing. They are pictures of the rules, not the
  toys' simulations.
- The flights, the night falling, the crossfades, the Earth mark, the bells.
