# Taste: rounds

One line a round, with the clock of each loop step (ET).

- R1 (Nov 4, 5:30–6:30 pm ET): a creature of eight numbers (colour, spikes, arms, curl, markings, second colour, glow, temper) drawn in one shader; a Metropolis walk whose target is the share of time the reader's finger rested on forms like it (tuned with pretend readers, `taste-taster`); a kept form chimes (and taps the hand while held), a turned-down one sighs; after 34 steps with 10 s held it pulls back to the wheel of every form, the path drawing itself with the longest-kept forms on it; pinch for the wheel any time, a touch to come back, shake for a new creature.
