# Screw: the rounds

One line a round (painting-company row 250): what the critic said was weakest, and what changed.
- R1 (polish): the first launch a grey, silent record until the sleeve; now the practice record is on the turntable from the start. (The critic also read the rewind as losing the screw; measured, it doesn't: the rewind lands before the voice comes in. The tests now print their numbers for the critic.) The needle's way back is a fading streak on the ring.
- R2 (polish): the record didn't visibly turn (vinyl is even all round); now a DJ's strip of tape marks the cue, with dust, scratches and worn arcs turning with it, so slowing, chops, the spin back and the brake show on the whole disc. The ring quieter (it read as a gauge), the finger's light and the sleeve clearer, one name for the practice record.
