# Salt Flat (was Ghost Jam): rounds

One line a round, with the clock of each loop step (ET). Ghost Jam's five rounds on the Nagoya ring
(painting-company row 218, Nov 4) are in `main`'s history at 3032c9a (`toys/jam/ROUNDS.md`).

- Row 244, brainstorm (Nov 6, ET): four takes (cars on open salt; scooters crossing at Ho Chi Minh City;
  a city's veins at night; a car park emptying), judged by a fresh critic: the salt flat first, its risk
  an aimless opening and rings that lock up. Built it with the night already 30 s under way and bored
  drivers who peel off, so rings form and open.
- R1 (Nov 6): cars on the Bonneville salt at night with no roads; each follows the tail lights ahead,
  keeps an IDM gap, turns no tighter than a car; tyre tracks drawn on a fading layer over the salt
  crust's polygons. Tap a car: it brakes and the cars behind stop. Drag a car: it follows the finger and
  its convoy after. Hold the open salt: a light the cars come to circle. Fixed on the way: gridlock
  (drivers braked for cars alongside and across; now only for what is truly ahead, and creep round),
  wanderers curling into tight loops (a slowly drifting heading of their own), cars crowding the middle.
- R2 (Nov 6; R1 critic "ok", weakest: the screen's edge became a ring road and the tracks a permanent scribble): the salt reaches 1.45 screens on every side, so convoys turn back out in the dark; tracks fade by half in 12 s; paler salt; a held light larger, each driver circling it on its own orbit; a led car ignores strangers' gaps. The light's galaxy of circling convoys appears.
- R3 (Nov 6; R2 critic "ok", weakest: not night (grey salt, smudgy headlights) and too crowded): near-dark salt, headlights drawn as cones of warm light reaching 18 m ahead, half as many cars (one to 1,700 m²); a led car rides the finger like on a string (a finger moves faster than any car) and pulls out of its convoy; the plan's drags are real drags (its `move` events had teleported the finger).
- R4 (Nov 6; R3 critic "oh huh neat", weakest: the tap's red lost among ordinary brake lights and junction knots): brake lights only for a hard stop behind the car you follow (2.2 m/s²) or standing; easing off round a stranger shows only on an emergency stop (a test holds untouched red under 4% of cars); new leaders only going your way and not cut in under your nose; strangers seen 26 m ahead and swerved round; a tap holds the car 2 s so the chain behind it forms; the view 20% closer (cars read), one car to 1,300 m².
- R5 (Nov 6; R4 critic "oh huh neat", weakest (not polish): the first sight a tangle in the middle, the headlights one beige smog): the night opens on a few long convoys laid across the whole salt, 12 s under way, with a few loners; one car to 1,700 m²; headlight cones tighter and dimmer.
