# Fireflies: rounds

One line a round: what changed, and the clock of each loop step (ET).

- Round 1 (Nov 3, 2026): the night Earth's 5,063 lit patches (Black Marble) each a Mirollo–Strogatz firefly; cities fall into step with neighbours within seconds, regions flash as one and waves sweep continents; a tap sparkles the lights under the finger out of step and the field pulls them back. Plans `start` (first sight, 24 s film) and `nudge` (close over North America, two taps).
