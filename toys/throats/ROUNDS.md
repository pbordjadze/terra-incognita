# Throats: rounds

One line a round: what the critic saw (from filmed renders), the weakest point, and what came next.
- Round 1 (zoom proof; film: open, pinch out, pinch in). Lizards read at 1:1 at the opening (7.5 pt a rock) but the colours are ragged patches, not spirals; halfway out (about 5 pt) both read, "the most beautiful view". Weakest: the opening frame hides the idea. Fully out, the hill repeats as a tile and the blues go lavender (paisley wallpaper). More, not polish; ship no. Next: open at the middle scale, no tile, truer far colours, arms that visibly turn.
- Round 2 (open at 5 pt, a stroke, pinch out and in). Real spiral hooks that visibly curl from the first seconds; the far view "the most beautiful frame", no tiling; close view works. Weakest: not a hillside with animals on it: an even lattice of lizards like knitted fabric, uniform ground, nothing reads as sun. The pour reads as a soft swath. More; ship no. Next: scatter the lizards over real terrain, unevenly, rocks mattering.
- Round 3 (grass meadows between rocky patches; opening on the most rocky window). Spirals crisp and curling when shrunk, the pour shows as a band by 7 s and curls into new arms by 11.5 s. Weakest: still not animals living on a hillside: wall-to-wall even density, rocks empty islands, cool grey ground, a halftone dot grid fully out. More; ship no. Next: thin the lizards, rocks as their places, ground between them, no grid at any zoom.
- Round 4 (stones scattered by noise, half the lizards out). Sunnier, warmer, real clearings; the far view "superb: crisp, saturated, curling arms between grass islands and boulders". Weakest: still a carpet, lizards packed and overlapping like a school of fish, ignoring the rocks. More; ship no. Next: far fewer lizards, each on its rock, the spirals read from the colours; open further out.
- Round 5 (one lizard on its own rock for every 7x7 territories, the territories' colours washed over the ground). One lizard a rock reads well up close; the pour is a clear band at 7 s and new curling arms by 9–11.5 s. Weakest: the colour is painted onto the ground (lavender gravel); fully out a paisley of washes; grass patches with square corners. More; ship no. Next: no wash, the lizards alone carry the spirals; round grass edges.
- Round 6 (each territory one lizard on its own small rock, 12 pt apart; no colour wash anywhere; far out each lizard a speck). The fully-out view "superb: crisp, saturated, curling arms in confetti points, no paint wash, warm grass islands with round natural edges, boulders the arms flow around; the best frame in six rounds". Weakest: the opening at 12 pt is a wall-to-wall swarm (bodies read as wasps as much as lizards) with no visible spiral, and the pour doesn't show as a band at that scale. More; ship no. Next: open at the far-to-middle scale, the close view the reward for zooming.
- After round 6 (the loop's limit of six rounds; not judged by a critic): the hill opens at 3 pt a lizard, the scale round 6 called the best frame, so a pinch in finds the lizards. Shipped as throats-build.
