## Round 1 · 2026-11-13 05:30 ET · build: branch claude/project-thread-0qnxke (uncommitted) · critic: fresh subagent

- First impression: an animated topo chart, not dusk on a big sea; the bending is real and easy to read (crests fanning round the dark wedge of the canyon), but every crest the same thin grey hairline, the coast one uniform pale band with sawtooth notches, dark land filling 40% of the screen, the town orange confetti, nothing warm on the water.
- Weakest thing: the peak doesn't happen where the story says; the brightest crests are mid-water, the white water off Praia do Norte like the rest of the coast; sets barely show; the short swell turns to shimmering moiré.
- The one change: make height visible and local: dim fine lines in the open sea, the Praia do Norte peak a thick bright foaming wall in a set, the rest of the coast's white water quiet; frame on the peak and the canyon.
- Ship as is? No.
- Done for round 2: the brightness goes as the waves' energy (the open sea dim); the surf as white as the swell is focused there, so Praia do Norte's beach foams and the bay in the canyon's lee barely; near breaking where focused, the face stands up into a bright wall; crests too fine for the screen fade to their mean; the afterglow's warmth on the western water and crests; the view framed on Praia do Norte and the canyon's head; fewer, smaller town lights and a stronger lighthouse.

## Round 2 · 2026-11-13 05:55 ET · build: branch claude/project-thread-0qnxke (uncommitted) · critic: fresh subagent

- First impression: much better, still a dark nautical chart with animated contour lines more than dusk over a big sea; the surf gathers into white sawtooth patches; the town, lighthouse and the canyon's calm wedge read clearly.
- Round 1's point: partly met. Brightness varies, the canyon is calm, the frame centres on the headland; but the brightest white sat well north of the headland, the sets barely showed, the short swell streaked like rain on glass, the land still 45% of the screen.
- Weakest thing: nothing is the hero, no giant wave and no spray; the surf's hard spikes and the blocky coastline look like bugs; dark blue blobs inland look like mistakes.
- The one change: make the Praia do Norte peak an event: a set's crest thickening across the shelf, standing up into a lit wall by the headland and bursting into spray that lingers; quiet between sets; the surf's spikes softened.
- Ship as is? No.
- Done for round 3: the sets sharper and the lulls quiet (the envelope cubed); a set's big wave at the peak throws spray along Praia do Norte that drifts landward and lingers (with its crash); the coast drawn smooth between land and sea; the surf's edge softened; the inland water no swell reaches drawn as land; the view shifted so less land shows.
