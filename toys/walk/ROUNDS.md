# Night Walk: rounds

One line a round, with the clock of each loop step (ET).

- R1 (Nov 4, 5:10–5:30 pm ET): the land (hills far apart, bumps on them), the walker on Metropolis's rule at 120 tries a second, its footprints' log drawn as moonlit relief with contours, dawn after 42 s with the land under the map, a new land each night; a touch sets the stride (the ring reaches the finger); footfalls by step length, wind, crickets, birds at dawn; tests hold the map to the land and the strides to their feel.
