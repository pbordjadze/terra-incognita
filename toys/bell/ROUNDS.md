# Bell: rounds

One line a round: what changed, and the clock of each loop step (ET).

- Round 1 (Nov 3, 2026): the Earth struck where a finger taps rings in PREM's 0S0, 0S2–0S12 (a bell's inharmonic chord, 0S2's singlets beating), heard at the place facing the reader; the surface swells and sinks in each degree's pattern about the strike, the outline squashes and breathes, the Rayleigh ring runs round the world and focuses at the antipode; idle, it rings from the great quakes (Valdivia first).
