# Barchans: the critics' rounds

Each round a fresh critic judges the toy from full-size screenshots only (not the code), told nothing of
the maker's reasoning. Its verdict, then what the maker changed. Times are ET.

## Round 1 (2026-11-13, ~1:25 AM ET, commit 305739b0): not shippable
- First look: a muddy field with streaks like rain on glass and pebble-like lumps; no crescent until
  twenty seconds and a drag; stripes across the dunes, soft smeared edges, black specks in the shadows;
  the poured pile with a black hole for a shadow; Terra's window muddy, centred over the sea.
- The one change asked: unmistakably a Tarfaya barchan field, many crisp crescents with horns
  downwind, sharp brinks and dark slip faces on pale ground, walking from the first frame.
- Changed: the rule now lets a hop stray across the wind and a landed slab creep to the lower side
  (lateral diffusion, after Momiji and others), which is what draws out horns; the field starts from
  young crescents and is run 1,000 sweeps into its own (a larger field, 256 x 560 cells, so more
  dunes); sand in transit no longer shows; the sun low from upwind so slip faces sit in shade; pale reg,
  bright sand, smoother heights, ambient light in the shadows; the light moved inland of the coast and
  its window paler, with slip faces.

## Round 2 (2026-11-13, ~2:00 AM ET, commit 7d819655): not shippable
- First look: real dunes on bare ground now, half of them crescents, but narrow horseshoes; a hard black
  slip-face shadow with a jagged edge; banding; after the wind turns, the dunes melt to commas and the
  shadows vanish (the sun didn't turn); the pour not to be found.
- The one change asked: crisp wide crescents back within seconds of any wind change, the light turning
  with the wind so the slip face always sits in soft shade; no banding.
- Changed: wider crescents (more spread across the wind); the heights softened by a small blur on the
  CPU (no banding); the sun low from upwind and turning with it; soft-edged shadows; a stronger pour, on
  bare ground in the plan.
