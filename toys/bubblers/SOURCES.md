# Bubblers: sources

What is real and what is drawn. The sources were found by web search on Nov 13, 2026 and are cited as
those results gave them. Bubblers loads no data: the beach and its crabs are drawn and run by code.

## Real

- **The crabs.** Sand-bubbler crabs (*Dotilla* and *Scopimera*) live on tropical sandy beaches across the
  Indo-Pacific and feed at low tide, sifting the surface sand for food and leaving the rest behind as
  small balls, in paths radiating from the burrow; the rising tide washes them away. Summarised from
  https://www.amusingplanet.com/2015/12/natures-sand-artist-sand-bubbler-crab.html and
  https://a-z-animals.com/articles/why-sand-bubbler-crabs-are-the-oceans-most-prolific-illustrators/.
- **The patterns as collective work.** "Visual art inspired by the collective feeding behavior of
  sand-bubbler crabs", https://arxiv.org/pdf/1709.00410: the radial paths of balls from each burrow and
  the way neighbouring crabs' patterns meet.
- **The place.** Phuket's west-coast beaches, in the crabs' range. The search found no study of the crabs
  at Phuket itself; the place is chosen, the crabs' habits are general.

## Drawn

Every rule's numbers: the feeding pace, a ball every second or so, how far a spoke reaches, choosing the
least sifted sand for the next spoke, the fear passed from crab to crab and its distances, the tide's
timing (minutes, not hours). The crabs' look, the sand, the ripples and the water.
