# Bubblers: the critics' rounds

Each round a fresh critic judges the toy from full-size screenshots only (not the code), told nothing of
the maker's reasoning. Its verdict, then what the maker changed. Times are ET.

## Round 1 (2026-11-13, ~1:25 AM ET, commit 305739b0): not shippable
- First look: sand like corduroy with square specks; the pattern already finished at the first frame;
  the balls in blobby clusters (lichen, not stars) around barely visible holes; after the tide, blocky
  water and a pegboard of round black holes; the furrow lies under the balls. The bolt cascade works.
- The one change asked: open on a freshly washed wet beach and let the crabs dig out and lay
  unmistakably radial spokes, so the starbursts fill the beach in front of you.
- Changed: it opens four seconds after the tide, wet and smooth, the burrows sealed until each crab digs
  out (and sealed again under the next tide); each spoke is one tight row of balls, the next a wider
  turn round; the crabs work faster so a starburst grows in a minute; round specks, gentler ripples,
  smoother water; a finger's furrow pushes the balls to its banks. A 45-second plan (`fill.json`) shows it
  left alone.

## Round 2 (2026-11-13, ~2:00 AM ET, commit 7d819655): not shippable
- First look: the radial spokes and the 45-second payoff now land; the balls like strung pearls, lighter
  than the sand, all alike; the holes flat ink dots; the tide a flat green sheet; faint banding.
- The one change asked: balls and holes made of the sand: wet-sand colour, jitter in size and place, a
  lit and a shaded side, paling as they dry; each hole a soft hollow with a raised collar.
- Changed: just that (balls of wet sand that pale over a minute, sizes 0.11–0.2 cm, off the line a
  little; a collar, a hollow and a hole); the water clear at its edge with glints; the ripples in
  patches.

## Round 3 (2026-11-13, ~2:30 AM ET, commit eeebf68b): not shippable "by a narrow margin", the last round
- First look: a real beach; by 30–45 s overlapping starbursts of shaded sand balls that look like
  photographs of bubbler beaches; the crabs cartoonish beside it; most out already at the first frame.
- The one change asked: the tide a thin transparent swash, the sand and balls visible through it, no
  confetti flecks, glossy sand behind it.
- Changed: the water much clearer (a tint, not a sheet), its glints soft and no flecks, just-soaked sand
  shining; it opens a second after the tide so the crabs are seen digging out. Done after three rounds;
  left as polish: the furrow's ruler edge, the crabs' cartoon eyes.
