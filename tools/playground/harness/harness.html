    Drag turns the globe; a drag that begins on something touchable is its pack's (with the one-finger scheme, every drag is the front pack's: shift-drag or right-drag turns). Scroll zooms. Every pack on the page has its doorway on the globe, alive; touching one starts that pack beside the others, a touch on a running pack's things brings it to the front, and ✕ ends the front one. A stand-in globe (Natural Earth, the app's colours), not the app's renderer; each pack's scripts run in a worker with what JavaScriptCore gives them and nothing else.
