# Huddle: the critics' verdicts

## Round 1 (Nov 13, 2026): more; no ship
It reads as a block, a lattice. Frost on every side, not the windward one; the walkers' way from
windward to lee can't be read; turning the wind doesn't visibly change which side suffers. Keep: the
ice and snow, the long shadows, the snow answering a drag at once, the view from above.

## Round 2 (Nov 13, 2026): more; no ship
The frost now sits windward and moves with a turned wind; the pale walkers filing round to the lee
answer round 1. Weakest: the penguins themselves, the same dark capsule with the same gold dashes in a
regular grid, so the huddle reads as a slab or fabric. Break the lattice: hundreds of separate warm
bodies, varied spacing and heading, gaps and swells. Keep: the windward frost, the walkers, the wind
turning at once, the ice, the shadows, the view from above.
